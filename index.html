About MeliMade

Little accessories, personal stories.

MeliMade is a personalized accessories venture based in Peru. It focuses on affordable, customizable pieces and meaningful gifts for students and young adults. The goal is simple: make it easier to express your style and celebrate the people and moments that matter.
